## Required wording revisions

### Question 1 — greenhouse gas

Keep **A — Carbon dioxide** as the correct answer. Replace the stem with:

> `Which of the listed gases is a significant greenhouse gas that contributes to human-driven global warming?`

The current explanation is then appropriate. This removes the overly broad implication that CO₂ is the sole or largest greenhouse effect contributor in all contexts.

### Question 10 — acid rain

Keep **A — Sulphur dioxide** as the correct answer. Replace the stem with:

> `Which pollutant gas is a major contributor to acid rain?`

Revise the opening explanation to:

> `Sulphur dioxide, particularly from burning sulfur-containing fuels and industrial processes, dissolves in atmospheric moisture and is oxidised to form sulphuric acid, a major component of acid rain. Oxides of nitrogen also contribute.`

This avoids suggesting that sulfur dioxide is the principal pollutant form from ordinary modern vehicle exhaust.
