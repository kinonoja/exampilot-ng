# WAEC and NECO: Official Free-Material Research Report

## Decision boundary

This report covers **research only**. No WAEC or NECO question has been generated, imported, paraphrased from a past paper, or inserted into ExamPilot.

The governing rule is deliberately narrow: ExamPilot may use only a material published by WAEC or NECO on the body’s own official web property and available without payment, login, or an unclear licence. Officially released prompts are not copied; they may be studied only for high-level clarity, scope, and assessment expectations. Any practice question later made from this research must be new, labelled **practice content**, and not presented as a past question.

> **Confirmed practical result:** WAEC has a usable free, official style-and-standards evidence base for General Mathematics, Chemistry, and Physics. NECO’s accessible official pages currently provide examination and delivery context only; its official syllabus and Chief Examiner’s Report are paid resources, not genuinely free public material.

## B. Third-party “free past question” claims — excluded

The following source category is **not used** for research, generation, validation, citations, or wording: websites, apps, document hosts, social posts, video channels, and downloadable files that claim to offer “free WAEC past questions,” “free NECO past questions,” free syllabus PDFs, answer compilations, or alleged examiner reports but are not hosted by WAEC or NECO themselves.

This exclusion includes results such as independent syllabus sites, study blogs, document-hosting copies, and “CBT practice” repositories. A free download is not evidence of an authorised licence. The risk is especially high where a resource reproduces live or historical question text, marking keys, or whole paper layouts. Such material must not be used even as a paraphrase source.

## Subject recommendation before any future generation

| Exam | Recommendation | Why | Can generation begin now? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **WAEC** | **General Mathematics, Chemistry, and Physics** | WAEC lists all three in its official candidate/subject context, and its own e-Learning pages exist for each. Chemistry and Physics have the strongest direct official commentary on expected examination standards; General Mathematics is an official core subject. | **Yes, after a separate topic-plan and review-sample approval.** Questions must be original practice items and avoid claims about a fixed WAEC paper structure that the free official sources do not establish. |
| **NECO** | **No subject should enter content generation yet under the user’s official/free-only rule.** | The official material currently accessible confirms the examination and dual-mode delivery, but not a free subject syllabus, question format, marking structure, or released sample. NECO’s own fee table says the syllabus and Chief Examiner’s Report are paid. | **No.** A defensible next step is to obtain an authorised NECO syllabus or written permission, then conduct the same source review before proposing subjects. |

## Honest future product framing

When WAEC original practice is eventually approved, its metadata and interface should state: **“Original WAEC-style practice content — not a WAEC past question.”** It should not claim to reproduce an official paper, section count, time allocation, mark value, or marking scheme unless that subject-specific element is confirmed in a current official free publication.

For NECO, the product should show **“NECO preparation coverage coming after official syllabus verification”** rather than presenting an imitation exam or inferred question mix.
