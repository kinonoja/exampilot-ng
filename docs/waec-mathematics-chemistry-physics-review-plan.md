# WAEC Review-Only Topic Plan: General Mathematics, Chemistry, and Physics

## Status and source boundary

This is a **review plan only**. It creates no question rows, no student-facing WAEC content, and no claim that it reproduces a WAEC paper. All future items must be original practice content marked **“WAEC-style practice — not a WAEC past question.”**

The source boundary remains strict. This plan draws only on WAEC’s own e-Learning and official subject-availability material. The available official public pages expose examiner comments and released assessment material, but do **not** provide a current full topic-by-topic syllabus for these three subjects. Accordingly, the coverage below is a conservative map of documented domains rather than a claim of a complete or fixed WAEC syllabus.[1] [2] [3] [4] [5] [6]

> **Generation gate:** The configured structured-question service is currently unavailable after returning a usage-exhausted response in the separate JAMB batch. The five review samples per subject are therefore **held**. They will not be drafted, labelled as AI-generated, or persisted until the same service is available again.

## Shared quality and format principles

WAEC’s e-Learning material repeatedly emphasises questions that are clear, concise, unambiguous, well spread, and accompanied by clear rubrics.[2] [3] The future original practice items should therefore use self-contained stems, stated values/assumptions for calculations, one unambiguous answer, and concise explanations that teach the tested principle. They must not claim a specific official section count, time allocation, mark value, or marking scheme without a current subject-specific public WAEC source.

The Physics examiner material adds a distinct emphasis on simple apparatus, data collection and manipulation, graphing and interpretation, experimental procedure, theoretical background, and deductions. The Physics review sample should therefore include at least one data/practical-reasoning item, but it must remain an original practice question rather than a recreation of a released task.[6]

## General Mathematics: proposed review coverage

Official Mathematics examiner commentary demonstrates financial calculations, algebraic reasoning, geometry/mensuration, statistics, sequences, bearings, and rate/time interpretation across the released resource set.[3] [4]

| Review topic | Officially evidenced domain | Proposed review-only sample focus | Planned difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Financial mathematics | Compound-interest reasoning and multi-step interpretation | Original savings/interest scenario with explicit values | Medium |
| Algebra and equations | Roots, linear/quadratic formulation, and simultaneous reasoning | Original equation/model question | Medium |
| Geometry and mensuration | Plane figures, triangle properties, and area reasoning | Original geometry or measurement task | Medium |
| Statistics | Mean, range, and interquartile range | Original small-data interpretation question | Easy–medium |
| Bearings, distance, and rates | Diagram interpretation, bearings, and travel-rate relationships | Original navigation or rate-time problem | Medium–hard |

## Chemistry: proposed review coverage

Official Chemistry examiner resources demonstrate atomic structure and periodicity, acid-base quantitative chemistry, kinetic theory, organic chemistry and isomerism, electrolysis, energetics/fuels, and industrial processes.[5]

| Review topic | Officially evidenced domain | Proposed review-only sample focus | Planned difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Atomic structure and periodicity | Electron arrangement, ion formation, and periodic trends | Original structure/trend interpretation item | Medium |
| Quantitative chemistry and acid-base reactions | Balanced equations, concentration, and neutralisation | Original stoichiometry calculation with stated units | Medium–hard |
| Kinetic theory and gases | Assumptions of kinetic theory and real-gas behaviour | Original concept/application question | Medium |
| Organic chemistry and isomerism | Compound identification, preparation routes, structural/geometric isomerism | Original functional-group or isomer reasoning item | Medium |
| Electrolysis, energy, and industry | Electrochemical calculation, combustion/fuels, and industrial raw materials | Original calculation or process-principle item | Medium–hard |

## Physics: proposed review coverage

Official Physics examiner resources demonstrate dimensions, elasticity, projectile motion, satellites, kinetic theory, wave/particle properties of light, semiconductor electronics, rectification, and practical/data skills.[6] [7]

| Review topic | Officially evidenced domain | Proposed review-only sample focus | Planned difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Measurement and dimensional analysis | Units, dimensions, and formula reasoning | Original dimension/units check | Medium |
| Mechanics and motion | Projectile components and stated assumptions | Original motion/model question | Medium–hard |
| Elasticity and material behaviour | Strain energy and deformation | Original elasticity calculation or interpretation | Medium |
| Thermal, kinetic theory, and waves | Molecular motion, diffusion, and light phenomena | Original concept/application item | Medium |
| Space, electronics, and practical reasoning | Satellites, semiconductors, rectification, or data/graph interpretation | Original applied or data-reasoning item | Medium–hard |

## Approval gate after capacity returns

Once the generation service is available, each subject’s five samples should be generated and checked before any scale-up. The review request will ask specifically about stem clarity, explanation quality, distractor plausibility, calculation assumptions, and whether the proposed coverage is appropriately broad without impersonating a WAEC paper. No full WAEC batch should be generated or persisted without a separate approval after those samples are reviewed.
